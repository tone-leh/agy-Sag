- **Usage in `agy-Sag`**:
  - `packages/core/locales/ext/zh-CN.json` imports and adapts the Simplified Chinese UI dictionary and dynamic pattern templates (converted from `$N` capture syntax to `{N}` templates and deduplicated against `agy-Sag`'s built-in 465-entry core pack).
